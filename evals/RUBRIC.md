# Labelling rubric: can this candidate realistically apply?

For each posting, decide whether THIS candidate could realistically apply and be considered.

## Hard miss (cannot be fixed by effort this month)
- The posting needs citizenship or a security clearance, or says the candidate must ALREADY have the right to
  work in a country where this candidate has no work rights, or says there is no visa sponsorship.
- It is remote but limited to countries or regions that do not include the candidate's country.
  A job listed as "Remote" next to one country (for example "Remote - US", "Remote, Canada") is remote within
  that country only, unless the text clearly says candidates from anywhere are welcome.
- The candidate must be enrolled at a university in a different country.
- It is an internship based in the United States and the candidate has no US work rights.
- A PhD is required.
- It requires 2 or more years of experience beyond what the candidate has, or it is clearly a
  senior / staff / lead / principal / manager role.

## Soft miss (a strong candidate sometimes gets past it)
- It requires about 1 year more experience than the candidate has.
- It is on-site or hybrid in a country where the candidate has no work rights, and the posting does not say
  whether it sponsors visas.
- A Master's degree is required.
- The stated graduation year is one year off from the candidate's.

## Verdict
- `can_apply`: no hard miss and no soft miss.
- `stretch`: no hard miss and exactly one soft miss.
- `cannot`: any hard miss, or two or more soft misses.

Use only what the posting says. If the posting does not state a requirement, do not count it as a miss.
"Preferred", "nice to have" and "bonus" items are not requirements.

## Notes added after the first labelling round (18 Nov 2026)
These did not change any stored label. They record where the rubric was unclear:
- "It is an internship based in the United States" should not count as a hard miss when the posting itself
  says it sponsors visas for the role.
- Experience: within about half a year of the stated minimum counts as meeting it.
